
# Pass the Sea

### Starting formation
Facing Couples

### Dance action
All Pass Thru; Face your Partner; Step to a Left-Hand Wave.

### Ending formation
Left-Hand Ocean Wave

>
> ![alt](pass_the_sea.png)
>

### Timing
6

### Comment
The Ocean Wave Rule applies to Pass the Sea
