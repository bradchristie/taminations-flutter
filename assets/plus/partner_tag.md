
# Partner Tag

### Starting formation
a Couple or Mini-Wave

### Dance action
Dancers Turn to Face each other and Pass Thru.

### Ending formation
Back-to-Back Dancers before after

>
> ![alt](partner_tag.png)
>

### Timing
3

## Left Partner Tag
Dancers Turn to Face each other and Left Pass Thru.
