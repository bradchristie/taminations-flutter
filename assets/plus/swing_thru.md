
# Swing Thru or Left Swing Thru

In the Mainstream Program,
the starting formation for
Swing Thru or Left Swing Thru must be either 
a Wave of at least 3 dancers or an Alamo Ring.
At Plus it is also proper for the starting formation
to be a Right-Hand or Left-Hand Thar.
In addition, Swing Thru or Left Swing Thru
can also be called when the dancers are in a formation
that will become one of the above formations
when the [Facing Couples Rule](../ms/facing_couples_rule.md) is applied. 

When Swing Thru or Left Swing Thru starts from a Thar,
all dancers form one group. When
applying “those who can Turn 1/2” to the Center Four dancers,
the Center Four dancers Turn the Star 1/2.

For example, Swing Thru from a Right-Hand Thar
(that is, a Wrong Way Thar): All Turn 1/2
by the Right; the Center Four dancers Turn the Star
(by the Left) 1/2. The ending formation is a Right-Hand Thar.
