# Reverse
A prefix to a call that generally instructs dancers to interchange the directions of clockwise and
counterclockwise (and Right and Left) throughout the call. For the Plus calls where the prefix
“Reverse” can be used, a brief description of that application is included in the definition (Reverse
Circle to a Line and Reverse Dixie Style to a Wave). At Plus these uses are Extended
Applications.

In addition to the common Mainstream uses of Reverse Flutterwheel and Reverse Wheel Around,
Reverse can also apply to the Mainstream calls Half Sashay, Rollaway, and Single Circle to a
Wave
