
# Prefer

Used to designate a dancer or dancers different from those specified
in the definition. Two common uses are: from General Lines with Boys
facing on the ends, "Prefer the Head Boys, In Roll Circulate". From a
General Line with the Boys and at least one Girl facing the same way,
"Prefer the Boys, Explode the Line" (that is, the Boys step forward and all
Face and Pull By).
